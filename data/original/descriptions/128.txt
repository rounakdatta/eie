The world is complex, everything is in flux, no man steps in the same river twice. It’s been a fun ride, and it’s time to say goodbye.

Welcome to Episode 128 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

Amit and Ajay say goodbye to the show in this episode, and tell you why they are stopping.
