Welcome to Episode 2 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah. New episodes release every Friday. Please like and subscribe!

In this episode, Amit and Ajay talk about artificial intelligence. How will it change the world? How excited should we be? How scared should we be? Also discussed: pioneering feminists, pioneering monsters and pioneering masala chai.
