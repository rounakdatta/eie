Humanity progresses when people are free. India still hasn't learnt that lesson. 

Welcome to Episode 10 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this episode, Amit and Ajay talk about how freedom is the only route to prosperity -- and India has suffered for decades from its lack of freedoms of all kinds. They trace India's economic history from before Independence to the present day, examining the peaks & troughs in between.
