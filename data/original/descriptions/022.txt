Firms tend to grow, and even innovate, along predictable paths. But sometimes a firm does something truly audacious, involving a leap of imagination that seems to come out of thin air. Today's episode is about three such great leaps.

Welcome to Episode 22 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this episode, Ajay discusses three great leaps by three great firms: Sun Microsystems, Google and Amazon. Amit talks about how such leaps of imagination are more an act of creative storytelling than linear business logic. 

What is your story about yourself? How could you reimagine it?
