We could all be many things, but we often allow ourselves to get boxed into one version of us. And we don't even realise this.

Welcome to Episode 3 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this episode, Amit and Ajay talk about the dangers of allowing yourself to be frozen into one version of you. They also discuss how the process of investigation & prosecution need to be fixed in India. If the incentives are right, it will not be easy for a party in power to harass its opponents using the law. Also, Amit recommends important primers on classical liberlism, and Ajay discusses why Hydroponics are problematic and should be cancelled.
