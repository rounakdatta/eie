Are we slaves to the things that own us? To find ourselves, do we need to lose the mess around us? Do we need to declutter?

Welcome to Episode 30 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this conversation, Amit and Ajay share their philosophies towards decluttering -- and towards things. What should we buy? What should we give away? Do we need... stuff? Can Bengalis run?
