Politics attracts a particular kind of creature — which is why accidental politicians, who never meant to be in that game, are a curious breed.

Welcome to Episode 108 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this episode, Amit and Ajay relate the stories of five inspiring accidental politicians — and discuss the pros and cons of their situation!
