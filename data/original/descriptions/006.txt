Frédéric Bastiat taught us to seen the Unseen effects of our actions. Friedrich Hayek showed us how economies and societies really function. 

Welcome to Episode 6 of Everything is Everything, a weekly podcast hosted by Amit Varma and Ajay Shah.

In this episode, Amit and Ajay discuss the powerul ideas of two great Freddies. We learn how our government, like most goverments, redistributes FROM THE POOR TO THE RICH. We understand how economies work -- not through central planning, but the decentralised coordination of the price system. Amit and Ajay also recommend their favourite books for understanding how markets work.
